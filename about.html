About LABTRACK

Learn more about our smart laboratory management system

Who We Are

LABTRACK is a College Laboratory Management System developed to make laboratory activities easier, faster and more organized.

The system provides a centralized platform to manage laboratory equipment, student information, equipment issue and return records, and availability details.

It helps laboratory administrators maintain accurate records and monitor equipment usage efficiently.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify laboratory management by providing a user-friendly digital platform for maintaining equipment records, student details and laboratory transactions.

👁 Our Vision

Our vision is to create a smart and organized laboratory environment where equipment can be monitored, managed and tracked efficiently through modern technology.
